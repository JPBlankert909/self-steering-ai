# Self-Steering AI

Self-Steering AI is a paradigm for building artificial intelligence systems that don’t just optimize toward fixed goals, but can evolve their own goals safely over time.

Traditional AI is like a thermostat: it follows a fixed setpoint no matter what. Self-steering AI is more like a human: it can reflect, adapt, and adjust its priorities when the world changes — all while staying within safety constraints.

This repository explores:

Core framework: mathematical formalization of dynamic goals

Architectures: neural steering operators, safety monitors, multi-agent systems

Applications: healthcare, robotics, finance, autonomous vehicles

Safety & ethics: mechanisms for alignment, oversight, and interpretability

🚀 Why it matters: fixed-goal AI breaks under real-world complexity. Self-steering AI can self-correct, adapt, and remain aligned — making it not just more capable, but safer.
